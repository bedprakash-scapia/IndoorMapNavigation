Zones, not just distance. An airport is not one navigable space. A boarding gate is airside, a check-in desk is landside, a baggage belt is arrivals — and you may only move between them through a control point, in one direction. This app refuses routes that would cross illegally (try a baggage belt to a departure gate) and makes the legal crossing an explicit step.

How the zones are worked out. The published map does not label airside or landside, so zones are inferred: POIs that can only exist in one zone act as seeds, and those labels grow through the walkway network but cannot spread past a security POI. Terminals come from each feature's own building tag, so T1 and T2 are never mixed even though their map tiles overlap.
