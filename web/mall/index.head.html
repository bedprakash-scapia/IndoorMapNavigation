How this was made. Nothing here was typed in by hand except the shop names. The dark concourse was lifted out of the image, thinned to its centre line, and turned into a graph of 1,655 points across two floors. Every shop is a colour region whose category comes from the legend printed on the sheet. The two floors are joined at four escalator shafts, matched by atrium name. Distances are approximate — a drawing has no scale, so one is assumed.
