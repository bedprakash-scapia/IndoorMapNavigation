Everything runs in this page. The image never leaves your browser: the dark concourse is flood-filled out of it, thinned to the line a person actually walks, and turned into a graph; every shop is a colour region, and its category comes from the legend printed on the sheet. Only the names have to be typed. Distances are approximate — a drawing carries no scale, so one is assumed.
